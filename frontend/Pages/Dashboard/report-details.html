Detailed Report

At approximately 14:30 hours, Officer Martinez observed an individual engaging in suspicious behavior near the downtown commercial district. The subject was observed taking multiple photographs of building entrances and security camera positions. When approached, the subject quickly departed the scene heading north on 5th Avenue.

The individual appeared to be conducting surveillance of the area. Multiple witnesses confirmed the subject had been in the area for approximately 45 minutes prior to officer arrival. Security footage from nearby businesses has been requested and is pending review.

Recommend increased patrol presence in the area and follow-up investigation to identify the individual.
